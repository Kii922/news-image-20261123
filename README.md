World News — 23 November 2026. One 1024×1024 front page summing up Monday
23 November 2026 in 32 stories across 6 continents: 12 illustrated lead
cards with sublines, a numbered-pin world map with legend, and briefs 13–32.

Leads: COP31 collapses without a deal in Antalya · Democrats take 258 House
seats, the biggest majority since 1992 · Trump "inclined" to resume conflict
with Iran if no deal by New Year · 11 killed in strikes on the Kyiv region ·
Ukraine drone cripples the Russian corvette Merkury off Libya · tankers ablaze
in the Strait of Hormuz · the AI rout deepens (Nasdaq's worst week since
2020) · 24 dead in an Indonesia factory fire · Delta delays 1,600 flights in
Atlanta · Liverpool thrash Manchester United 4–0 in Pochettino's debut ·
Belgian airports shut by an austerity strike · the penny's 232-year run ends.

Every story was checked against a real outlet on 23 November 2026 (CNN,
Associated Press, Reuters, Al Jazeera, Xinhua, Inshorts, The Economic Times,
TBS News). Unconfirmed claims are worded as claims; nothing in the image is
from before 13 November 2026.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` byte for
byte to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`out/entry.png` is then byte for byte this repository's `entry.png`.
